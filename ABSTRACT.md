# Abstract

Concrete Crack Images for Classification is a curated packaging of a concrete crack dataset released on Mendeley Data for reproducible benchmarking.

The source data contains 40,000 RGB image patches at 227x227 resolution split equally into cracked (`Positive`) and non-cracked (`Negative`) classes, generated from 458 high-resolution (4032x3024) photographs of concrete building walls and floors on the METU campus (Ankara, Turkey) with varying surface finishes (exposed concrete, plaster, paint) and illumination conditions.

This package preserves publication metadata and adds generated statistics and visualization artifacts for the Large Road Damage DataLake.
